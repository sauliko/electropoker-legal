ElectroPoker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the ElectroPoker mobile game ("the app", "we") handles. ElectroPoker is published by Shaul Carmel. If you have questions, contact us at electropokerapp@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • Your credit balance is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers to show and measure ads.
  • ElectroPoker is a game for entertainment only. It does not involve real-money gambling.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your credit balance locally on your device so it is still there when you reopen the app. We do not receive this data and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch an ad to earn free credits"). To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and operating system details, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information from Google in a way that identifies you.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EU/EEA, UK): if you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time using the "Privacy & Ad Choices" link inside the app.
  • iOS: the app asks permission before tracking. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

5. In-app purchases and virtual credits

You can optionally buy packs of virtual credits. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. Credits have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes, and are for use inside the game only.

6. No real-money gambling

ElectroPoker is a simulated card game for entertainment. Playing it does not offer a chance to win real money or prizes. Success at simulated gambling does not imply future success at real-money gambling.

7. Children

ElectroPoker is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Data sharing, security, and retention

We do not sell personal information. Because the app does not collect personal information itself, there is nothing for us to share, and the only data we hold (your credit balance) stays on your device. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's own policies and retention practices.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the use of your personal information. Since we do not hold personal information about you, you can exercise most controls directly through the settings described above or by contacting Google about data it collects. You may also contact us at the address above with any request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
